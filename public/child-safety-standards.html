Child Safety Standards & CSAE Zero-Tolerance Policy

App: Mingle (AI Dating & Virtual Companion) | Effective Date: October 16, 2026 | Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1 Zero-Tolerance Prohibition on Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Absolute Prohibition: Mingle maintains an unconditional zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

Under no circumstances does Mingle tolerate, allow, or facilitate:

  • Any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Depiction, discussion, or generation of sexualized content involving minors or characters resembling minors.
  • Child grooming, solicitation of minors, or age-inappropriate roleplays.
  • Any attempt to jailbreak or guide AI personas into producing minor-involving sexually explicit or suggestive text, voice, or imagery.

Any account found engaging in, facilitating, or promoting such behavior will be permanently terminated immediately without refund and reported to relevant legal authorities and child protection organizations, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

2 Age Restriction: Strictly 18+ Application

Mingle is exclusively designed, intended, and licensed for adult users aged 18 and older. Minors are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, accessing AI companions, or utilizing virtual dating services on the platform.

All AI personas presented on Mingle are explicitly modeled and restricted to adult profiles (18+), and safety system prompts enforce this boundary universally.

3 In-App Feedback & Safety Reporting Mechanism

In full compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, Mingle provides a prominent, direct in-app feedback and reporting mechanism without leaving the app:

  • On Every Companion Profile: A persistent Shield button (🛡️) allows users to report content, abuse, or child safety violations immediately.
  • In Every Chat Screen: A dedicated 3-dot overflow menu provides a one-tap "Report / Child Safety" report sheet.
  • During Every Voice Call: An in-call Safety button enables instant reporting without interrupting or ending the connection abruptly.
  • In App Settings: Under "Safety, Child Protection & Feedback", users can submit detailed reports, upload evidence, and request human moderator intervention.

All safety reports submitted through the in-app mechanism are prioritized as critical severity and reviewed by our designated moderation team within 24 hours.

4 Automated AI Safeguards & Continuous Content Filtering

Mingle implements multiple layers of automated defense to protect users and guarantee compliance:

  • Real-time NLP Safety Classifiers: Every prompt and message is screened before AI generation. Requests relating to minors or CSAE are rejected instantly at the model inference layer.
  • Voice Output Guardrails: On-device and server-side speech synthesis are governed by filters that prevent vocalization of prohibited themes.
  • Automated Suspension: Repeated violations trigger automated account suspension and safety team notification.
